This Edwards Avenue project pairs two mirrored homes under one roofline, painted brick with black windows and black French entry doors, and stained wood porch posts at each entrance. Inside, we are running a warm, neutral palette with white oak floors, honed stone counters in the kitchen, and a freestanding soaking tub in the primary bath. Each home gets three additional bedrooms with their own bath. This one is currently in design and finish selection, with construction starting soon.
